Privacy

privacy approach.

How this informational website handles enquiries and basic visitor information.

Website enquiries

SS Circuit Products provides a secure website enquiry form and direct contact links. Enquiry information is transmitted to our Cloudflare-hosted processing endpoint and transactional email provider so it can be forwarded to the SSCP sales team. The website does not provide a public database of enquiries.

If you choose to send an email or WhatsApp message, your communication is handled by the relevant messaging provider, and information you send to SSCP may be used to respond to your enquiry.

Hosting and technical data

This website is designed for Cloudflare Pages hosting. Cloudflare may process network and security-related information as part of hosting the site. SSCP has not included third-party advertising trackers or analytics scripts in this website package.

Contact

For privacy questions about communication with SSCP, write to sales@sscircuitproducts.com.

If you have a question about how we handle information you share with us, please contact our team before sending sensitive documents.

Website enquiry submissions

When you submit the contact form, we process the details you provide (name, email, company, phone, product interest, plating requirements, message and optional drawing or specification) through our Cloudflare-hosted form handler and a transactional email delivery provider so our sales team can receive and respond to your enquiry. Any attached PDF or image is included in the email forwarded to our sales inbox. We do not keep a separate upload database. Cloudflare Turnstile also processes verification information to reduce spam. We do not use the form to register you for marketing messages.

For questions about personal information, please write to sales@sscircuitproducts.com.
